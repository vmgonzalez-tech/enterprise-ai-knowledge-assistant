EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK - DEMO COMPANY

ANNUAL LEAVE POLICY
Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave plus UK bank holidays.
Annual leave requests must be submitted at least 14 days in advance.
Employees can carry over a maximum of 5 unused days into the following year.

REMOTE WORK POLICY
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.
Remote working arrangements require manager approval.

EXPENSE POLICY
Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days.
Expenses over GBP 500 require prior manager approval.
